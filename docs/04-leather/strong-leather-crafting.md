# Strong Leather Crafting

Made with **Armorsmithing** from [Raw Leather](./raw-leather.md). Strong Leather is a base material for armor, including the Orichalcum set (which also needs metal from [Ore - Orichalcum](../03-gathering/nodes/ore-orichalcum.md) and catalysts).

| Recipe | Armorsmithing level | Input → Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strong Leather | 1 | 2 Raw Leather → 1 Strong Leather |
| Strong Leather ×10 | 25 | 20 Raw Leather → 10 Strong Leather |

- The level 25 batch recipe replaces five runs of the level 1 recipe at the same ratio.
- Decide which armor you will craft **before** spending your Raw Leather stock.
- Craft at the tables in major cities. Catalysts come from the nearby vendor for Kina.
- See [Crafting Professions](../05-crafting/crafting-professions.md).
