# Leather Farming by Level

Drop chances are flat per mob (no level-gap penalty found, see [Level Difference](../02-leveling/level-difference.md)), but farm **within about 10 levels** of your character to be safe and efficient. Rates below are the post-Oct 7 values where known. Raw Leather is only usable from **level 15**, so stockpile from then on.

## Elyos (Verteron)

Named mobs have one spawn and long respawns, so treat them as bonus kills (bring a full party of level 45+).

## By level band
- **Before 15:** you cannot use Raw Leather yet. Pick up Swarm/Lupyllini (Asm) or Cantas Valley beasts (Ely) as you quest.
- **15–25:** Dredgion/Moslan (Asm), Pilgrim's Ridge (Ely).
- **26–35:** Battlescar Mound Lupyllini and Tog (Asm). Elyos Tolbas and Aullaeu forests have beast mobs but I have no confirmed rate for them.
- **35–40:** Giant Ore Insectoid Adult at Fafnite Deposit (Asm), a good pairing with 180 Orichalcum nodes.
- **40–45:** Garden of the Illusion God Confused Ursus (Ely). Then the named level 45 mobs.
- **45+:** Abyss Swarm Colony in Lower Reshanta and everything scales to 45.

:::tip[Combine with ore]
Dredgion Crash Site and Fafnite Deposit give ore **and** leather, see [Gathering by Level](../03-gathering/gathering-by-level.mdx).
:::
