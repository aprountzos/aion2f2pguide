# Raw Leather

Raw Leather is **not a gathering node**. It drops from monsters (140+ monsters, levels 10–53) and feeds [Strong Leather Crafting](./strong-leather-crafting.md) for armor.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Grade | Common |
| Required level | **15** |
| Max stack | 10,000 |
| Vendor price | 9 Kina (treat as a floor) |
| Trade | Tradable. *Raw Leather (Bound)* is a separate untradeable item |

:::warning[F2P note]
Most guides say "buy it from the Market". Free players cannot buy there. Plan on farming, see [Free-to-Play Notes](../01-basics/free-to-play-notes.md).
:::

## Drop rates (per kill)
**Oct 7, 2026 update:** Raw Leather drop chance raised on **128 creatures** in Verteron, Altgard and Chaotic Lower Reshanta.

| Creatures | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 90 | 3.9% | **5.8%** |
| 18 | 0.97% | 1.5% |
| 8 | 1.9% | 2.9% |
| 7 | 4.7% | 6.1% |
| 4 | 3.5% | 4.5% |
| 1 | 11.7% | 15.2% |

Regional baselines (from a guide updated Oct 9, may predate the increase):

| Region | Typical per-kill |
| --- | --- |
| Verteron / Altgard | ~3.9% (now 5.8% for most common mobs) |
| Chaotic Lower Reshanta | 3.5%–8.4% |
| Chaotic Middle Reshanta | 4.7%–11.7% |
| Eltnen / Morheim | ~7.8% |

Mob families that drop it: Ursus, Zaif, Slink, Tog, Karnif, Coradon, Brax, Swarm.

## Other sources
- **Shulak Loot Bundle (Bound):** 14.8% Raw Leather per opening.
- **Supply Request:** turn in 5 Raw Leather for 380 Abyss Points + 100 Enhance Stones. Keep what your armor recipe needs first.
- Abyss Swarm Colony (Chaotic Lower Reshanta) was 8.4% before the update. Needs level 45.
