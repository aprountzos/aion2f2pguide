## Privacy, comments and reactions
This site has no accounts, no ads and no analytics of its own. When comments are switched on, the comment box under a page is provided by [giscus](https://giscus.app), which stores comments and 👍 👎 reactions as GitHub Discussions of the project's repository. Loading the box contacts giscus.app and GitHub, and writing a comment or reaction needs a free GitHub account and is subject to [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Comments are public. Do not post personal information.

## Where the data comes from
The numbers are gathered from community sites, interactive maps and wikis, and are listed on [Sources](../09-reference/sources.md). Facts such as levels, node counts and drop rates are summarized in our own words and tables. If you are the owner of a source and want a change or a credit, open an issue.
