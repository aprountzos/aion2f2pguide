# Enhancement and Upgrades

## The golden rule while leveling
**Do not heavily enhance gear you will replace.** Early leveling produces new gear constantly. Equip quest gear as you get it and save heavy investment for gear you plan to keep (around the level 45 cap).

## Enhancement (Growth)
| Level | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| 1–10 | **Guaranteed** success. Costs Kina and Enhance Stones |
| 10–15 | Success chance. A failure does not destroy gear or reduce level but the resources are lost |
| 15–20 | Needs **Amplify Stones** + Kina. Five breakpoints with lower rates, each failure raises the next chance |

- At level **10 or below** you can extract the stones back (all stones, not the Kina). Taking gear to +10 is therefore a cheap, reversible choice.
- Kina cost rises exponentially past +10, and failed attempts burn materials and Kina.
- **Potential:** adds PvE or PvP damage without changing the level.

## Other systems
- **Manastones:** engrave four extra stats for Kina. Rolls are random.
- **Theostones:** give a weapon a chance to add an effect (fire, poison, paralysis). Matter more for PvP.
- **Soul Binding:** adds four stats. *Bind* raises existing stats, *Sync* rerolls one stat (uses Unique gear), *Reset* rerolls all four (costly).
- **Transfer:** moves Growth level and Soul Binding to another item, but the old item loses Potential, Manastones and Theostones. Transfer Crafting does a similar thing at craft time.

## Getting Enhance Stones for free
1. **Sealed dungeons**, one-time: each home-faction clear for the 22 and 32 Ascensions gives **1,250 Enhance Stones** (see [Milestones 1-45](../02-leveling/milestones-1-45.mdx)). Do your rival faction's sealed dungeons after your own.
2. **Daily Dungeon** (Unknown Fissure): unlocks at level 30, 14 attempts per week.
3. **Extracting spare gear.**
4. **Supply Request:** 5 Raw Leather → 380 Abyss Points + 100 Enhance Stones ([Raw Leather](../04-leather/raw-leather.md)).

## What to buy with your stones
- Early: +10 on your main weapon or armor, nothing more.
- At 45: go to [After Level 45](../08-level-45/after-level-45.md).
