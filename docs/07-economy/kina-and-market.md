# Kina and Market

## Currencies
- **Kina**: earned in game. Two kinds: tradable, and **bound** (cannot be traded to other players).
- **Quna**: premium currency, bought with real money. Subscribers can exchange Quna for Kina player-to-player.

## Market
- Subscribers get the **Marketplace** (server market, later a cross-server market), the Exchange, and a subscriber shop.
- Fee structure: a listing fee plus about **10%** commission per sale (per one report). Exact amounts not published in my sources.
- Free players cannot buy. Whether they can list is disputed, see [Free-to-Play Notes](../01-basics/free-to-play-notes.md).
- Unbound Kina comes mainly from Market and exchange sales, available only to subscribers.

## Kina sinks
- Enchanting up to +15 (exponential past +10, failures burn Kina and stones), see [Enhancement and Upgrades](../06-gear/enhancement-and-upgrades.md).
- Crafting, magic stone engraving, soul engraving, repairs, catalysts.
- Gathering respec: 50,000 Kina.

## Tips
- Check whether an item is **bound or tradable** before selling to an NPC. Tradable items may be worth far more than the vendor price (Raw Leather vendor price is only 9 Kina).
- Keep potentially tradable materials instead of auto-selling or extracting them. Values change as you craft.
- Vendor-selling white or green gear is the better option when you want Kina instead of upgrade materials.
- Poor-stat gear can be extracted for materials.
