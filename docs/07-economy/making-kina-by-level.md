# Making Kina by Level

The sources give almost no exact amounts. The only exact figure is **Duty Missions: 50,000 bound Kina each, 5 paid completions per day**.

| Level | Main Kina sources |
| --- | --- |
| 1–20 | Story quests, Duties, green side quests. Most is **bound** Kina, spent on basic progression. Optional content gives small Kina but also gear and materials you would otherwise pay for |
| 20–40 | Kill ordinary monsters while doing other objectives: Kina, EXP, materials and gear drops. Side quests pay more as level rises. Sealed dungeons and strongholds give materials and Kina. Keep tradable materials |
| 40–45 | Keep following the story while farming. Do not spend on temporary upgrades. Level 45 bracelet quest gives 100,000 bound Kina |
| 45+ | Duty Missions, Odyle Energy cubes (Expeditions, Transcendence, Sanctuary), Abyss supply orders, weekly missions. See [After Level 45](../08-level-45/after-level-45.md) |

## Crafting income
- Consumables (food, potions, scrolls) have steady demand. Calculate materials + crafting cost + Market fees first.
- If you cannot access the Market, crafting is for **your own use**, not profit.

## Gathering income
- Without Market access you cannot sell Orichalcum, gems or logs for Kina. Use them for your own crafts instead, see [Crafting Professions](../05-crafting/crafting-professions.md).
- If you can list items, surplus materials from [Gathering by Level](../03-gathering/gathering-by-level.mdx) are your best low-risk sale.
