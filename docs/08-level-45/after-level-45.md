# After Level 45

Level 45 is the Global Season 1 cap. **All monsters scale to level 45**, so zone level no longer matters. Progression shifts from character level to **Item Level (gear score)**, Arcana, Daevanion boards, Pantheon and Abyss upgrades.

## Item level gates
| Item level | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| 700+ | Early Conquest (Krao Cave / Draupnir tier). **Draupnir clear needs 700** |
| 1,000 | **Chaotic Lower Reshanta** (Abyss), plus level 45 |
| ~1,400 | Next Conquest tier |
| ~1,600 | Transcendence begins (Arcana) |
| ~2,100 | Fire Temple / Ferocious Horn Den tier |
| 2,200–2,500 | Higher Transcendence tiers |
| 2,800 | Abyssal Forge: Ludra (10-player raid) |

## Systems to open
- **Regional quests:** rings, earrings, necklaces, PvE runes, titles. Replaces weak leveling gear.
- **Sealed dungeons:** one-time Skill Points and Daevanion resources.
- **Strongholds:** belt enhancement.
- **Empyrean Traces** (feathers): turn in at the Monolith for permanent progression. Raid Relay's map lists 560 feathers per continent.
- **Nightmare:** Phantasmal Fragments. Statues and Colossus for Pantheon.
- **Abyss:** weekly time budget per layer (7 h free).
- **Crafting:** profession upgrade quest at the first Mastery cap.

## Gathering at 45+
Go where nodes are densest and nearest a teleport. After the pet-soul gap disappears, farm leather in the Abyss Swarm Colony (Lower Reshanta) or Eltnen/Morheim at ~7.8% per kill. See [Raw Leather](../04-leather/raw-leather.md).
