# Crafting Professions

Five professions, each with a crafting table in the major cities. Mastery (like gathering proficiency) runs **Novice 1–50, Professional 51–100**. Complete the profession upgrade quest at the first Mastery cap (50). Crafting specialty quests appear around **level 10**.

| Profession | Makes | Recipes | Key materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Blacksmithing** | Melee weapons: swords, greatswords, daggers, maces, shields | 32 | Orichalcum Ore → Ingots, catalysts, Solvent |
| **Armorsmithing** | All armor: helmets, breastplates, leggings, gloves, boots, capes | 44 | [Strong Leather Crafting](../04-leather/strong-leather-crafting.md), metal, catalysts |
| **Handicrafting** | Bows, staves, necklaces, rings | 56 | Logs, gems, Odyle |
| **Alchemy** | Spellbooks, orbs, potions, scrolls, enhancement items, Mana Stones, Soul Stones | 58 | Herbs (recipe details not in my sources) |
| **Cooking** | Food and drink with temporary buffs | 32 | Cooking ingredients |

527 recipes in total across the five.

## Mechanics
- Every craft gives profession EXP. Higher levels unlock higher-tier recipes and improve success on hard crafts.
- **Crafting Combo:** a chance, when a normal craft succeeds, to replace the result with a combo result.
- **Transfer Crafting:** carries the item's Enhancement level, Amplify level and Soul Binding to the new item (see [Enhancement and Upgrades](../06-gear/enhancement-and-upgrades.md)).
- Batch crafting (for example 50 at once) is more efficient than many small crafts.
- Odyle ([Odyle](../03-gathering/nodes/odyle.md)) is used in most recipes, especially weapons, armor and Handicraft.

## F2P advice
- Pick the profession that matches **your own class's gear** and your gathering specialty: Orichalcum + leather for Armorsmithing, logs + Odyle for Handicrafting.
- Level crafting alongside gathering so you do not hit a material bottleneck, see [Gathering by Level](../03-gathering/gathering-by-level.mdx).
- Calculate material cost before crafting for profit. If the item sells for less than the materials, sell the materials instead.
- Use the crafting calculator on aion2.online to get the full shopping list.

## Sample route: first sword
6 Orichalcum Ore + 2 Odyle + 3 Solvent (buy) + 5 Refining Stones, per one wiki. Treat this as a rough guide.
