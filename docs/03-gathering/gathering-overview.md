# Gathering Overview

Gathering is called **Essence Extraction**. Walk up to a node (ore vein, plant, gem, floating Odyle orb) and interact.

## How an extraction works
- A window with two gauges appears: **Success** and **Failure**. Fill Success first to get the material. If Failure fills first, the extraction fails.
- Each node offers a three-slot interface (up to three harvests), per one guide.
- Monsters can interrupt you, so clear the area first.
- Nodes can give a **bonus item** on top of the material.
- Maps mark *possible* node locations, not guaranteed live spawns. If one is missing, move to the next marker.

## Starting out
- Gathering opens at **character level 10**, right after leaving your first hub.
- Gather **while questing**: hit nodes on your path instead of dedicated sessions. Even 15–20 minutes a day adds up.
- Gathering proficiency is separate from combat level.
- Use the map layers ([Zone Levels](../09-reference/zone-levels.mdx), maps in [Sources](../09-reference/sources.md)) to plan routes, see [Gathering by Level](./gathering-by-level.mdx).

## Proficiency
- **Lv. 1–100**: **Novice 1–50**, **Professional 51–100**.
- Proficiency **stops at 50** until you complete the promotion quest. More ordinary nodes will not help.
- Ore, gem and log nodes in the early zones need only Proficiency Lv. 1.

### Promotion to Professional
| Faction | NPC | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Asmodian | **Alzirr** (Safe Haven) | Gather **Ruby** in **Calderon Canyon** for a *Splendent Ruby (Bound)* and turn it in |
| Elyos | **Korius** | Details not confirmed. One guide mentions a side quest in "Verdderon Village" (probably a typo for Verteron) |

:::warning
Radiant Ruby Gemstone is a crafting material and **cannot** replace the quest item.
:::

You need Novice 50 for this, so keep gathering as you level. It will also be needed for the crafting professions' own upgrade quests, see [Crafting Professions](../05-crafting/crafting-professions.md).

## Skills
- **Proficient Handling** — fewer failures. **Delicate Touch** — faster gathering. **Lady Luck** — luck.
- Each material has its own skill line with **Seeker** skills that show nodes on the map (for example *Ore Seeker*, *Odyle Seeker*).
- Other tree skills: *Abundant Harvest* (more per extraction), *Odyle Appraiser* (higher quality Odyle). One guide says to reach the "double yield" skill early since it roughly halves the time per material.
- A respec costs **50,000 Kina**. Pick the specialty your own crafting needs first.
- A forum post suggests 10/10/5 (Handling/Touch/Lady Luck) early. It comes from a currency-selling site, so treat it as unverified.

## Grades
Ore, gems, logs and Odyle have four grades: **Standard, Fine, Pure, Radiant**. Herbs have regular and **Fresh**. Cooking ingredients have one grade.
