## Rules of thumb
1. **10–20:** ore (Dredgion Crash Site or the Cantas Valleys) and logs.
2. **18–30 Elyos:** live in Ellun River Midstream. **21–22 Asmodian:** Moslan Forest has ore and logs together.
3. **By Novice 50:** have enough Ruby for the promotion (Calderon Canyon for Asmodians), see [Gathering Overview](./gathering-overview.md).
4. **33–37 Asmodian:** Basfelt Ruins Ruby and Fafnite Deposit Orichalcum are the big clusters.
5. **45+:** go wherever is densest and closest, monsters scale to 45.
6. Pair with leather: [Leather Farming by Level](../04-leather/leather-farming-by-level.mdx).
