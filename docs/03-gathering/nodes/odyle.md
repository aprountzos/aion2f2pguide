# Odyle

:::warning[Not the same as Odyle Energy]
**Odyle** is a crafting material you gather. **Odyle Energy** is a refilling resource spent on reward cubes. See [Glossary](../../01-basics/glossary.md) and [Daily and Weekly Checklist](../../01-basics/daily-and-weekly-checklist.md).
:::

## What it is
- A **floating** gathering node (shimmering green orb). It is not on the ground, so you need **Wings** to reach it.
- Gathered with Essence Extraction. Four grades: Standard, Fine, Pure, Radiant.
- Found near camps, towns, cities and points of interest, and in the Expeditions. One guide also says you can extract it after a Conquest boss.
- Used in most crafting recipes, especially Weapon Forge, Armor Forge and Handicraft. The first-sword route needs 2 Odyle.

## Tips
- Wings mode has its own **stamina bar**. If it runs out mid-extraction, the extraction can be canceled. Check stamina before heading to a node.
- Skills: **Odyle Seeker** (shows Odyle on compass and minimap), **Abundant Harvest** (more per extraction), **Odyle Appraiser** (chance of higher quality).
- Wings unlock at level 5 (Elyos) or 6 (Asmodian), so you can gather Odyle from level 10 like everything else.
- Extract it when passing aerial nodes instead of making a trip.
