# Logs — Asvata and Yggdrasil

Gathered from **Proficiency Lv. 1**. Logs feed wood-based crafts (Handicrafting makes bows, staves and jewelry).

| Log | Faction side | Grades | Bonus drops |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Asvata** | Altgard (Asmodian) | Standard, Fine, Pure, Radiant | Seed of Detection, Poko's Skin Bundle (Bound) |
| **Yggdrasil** | Verteron (Elyos) | Standard, Fine, Pure, Radiant | Seed of Detection, Poko's Skin Bundle (Bound) |
