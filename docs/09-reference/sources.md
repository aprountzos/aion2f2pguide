## Known gaps and conflicts
- 17 (Wikily) vs 27 (Metabot) gathering node types: different grouping.
- Asmodian Ruby locations differ between Fextralife and AllThings.How.
- Calderon Canyon (10–14 vs 22) and Kraka Footpath (39–42 vs 45) levels differ between sources.
- Free players listing on the Market: reports conflict.
- No Aion 2 source found for how level gap affects EXP or Raw Leather drops. Only pet souls are confirmed.
- Regional Raw Leather baselines may predate the Oct 7 increase.
- The "10/10/5" skill split is from a currency-seller forum post, unverified.
- Herb/cooking recipes, Diamond per-area counts, and Asmodian gathering nodes in levels 26–31 are not covered.
