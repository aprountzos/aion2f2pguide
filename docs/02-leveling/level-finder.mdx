# Level Finder

Find your level band and faction. **Zone levels** come from Raid Relay's map data; mob levels from AllThings.How. See [Level Difference](./level-difference.md) for why this matters and [Zone Levels](../09-reference/zone-levels.mdx) for the full table.

Node counts in brackets. Nodes have no character-level requirement (only proficiency, which starts at Lv. 1), so the zone's **level range** is about how dangerous the monsters around the nodes are.

## Find your level
Type your level and pick your faction. The list below updates by itself from the guide's data (on the website). Without the website, use the level bands underneath.

## Browse by level band

### Levels 1–9 (intro)
- No gathering yet (starts at **level 10**).
- Wings at level 5 (Elyos) or 6 (Asmodian). Skill points from level 4.
- Elyos: Poeta (Daminu Forest, Clione Lake, Migzatu Brigade Base). Asmodian: Ishalgen (Munihelle Forest, Tunapre Lake, Azrakar Brigade Guard Camp).

### Level 45+
Monsters scale to level 45, so zone level no longer matters. Go where the nodes and drops are densest. See [After Level 45](../08-level-45/after-level-45.md).
