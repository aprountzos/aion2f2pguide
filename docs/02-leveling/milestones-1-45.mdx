# Milestones 1–45

<DataTable id="milestones" />

## Ascension tips
- The gauge fills from quests and exploration, **not** ordinary monster kills.
- Fill it with Regional quests first, then sealed dungeons.
- Sealed dungeons for the 22 and 32 Ascensions:
  - **Elyos:** Poacher's Den (10, Eastern Cantas Valley), Fortress Jail and Fortress Grave (20, Verteron Fortress Ruins), Kraka's Altar (30, Kraka Footpath).
  - **Asmodian:** Abandoned Memorial Room (Uruthumheim, around 22), Drana Depository (25, Dranactus).
- Each home-faction clear gives 2 Wisdom Stones, 2 Daevanion Crystals and **1,250 Enhance Stones**.

## Party dungeons on the way
Krao Cave (easy bosses 22), Urugugu Canyon (easy bosses 30), Fire Temple (37, Elyos story), Draupnir (45). Draupnir needs **700 gear score**.

## EXP
Total EXP to reach level 45 is 754,816,909 (levels 1–9 cost 89,148).
