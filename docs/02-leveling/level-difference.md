# Level Difference — Over-Leveled and Under-Leveled

You asked whether being higher level than a zone's monsters hurts. Here is what I could confirm and what I could not.

## Confirmed
- **Pet soul drops** fall off once the gap between you and the monster is **more than 10 levels**. Within 10 levels you get a soul from almost every kill; beyond it, roughly one per 20–30 kills. Example: a level 30 character gets almost nothing from a level 10 monster but nearly one per kill from a level 23 monster.
- At **character level 45, every monster scales to level 45**, so there is no gap and normal drops are restored.

## Not confirmed
- I did **not** find a source stating how level gap affects EXP, Raw Leather drop chance or Kina in Aion 2. Drop tables list one flat percentage per mob (for example 5.8%), with no mention of a penalty.
- Treat it as likely that low monsters give less EXP (as in most MMORPGs), but this is an assumption.

## Practical rules
1. For **leveling**, follow the story route. Zones are travel guidance, you do not have to finish each one.
2. For **gathering**, nodes have no level requirement. Gathering in a zone below your level is fine and safe, and a low zone's nodes are just as good (Orichalcum is Orichalcum).
3. For **leather and pet souls**, farm mobs within about **10 levels** of you, ideally near your own level.
4. Going **up** is the real risk: monsters around a higher-level zone's nodes can interrupt extraction and kill you. Clear the area first.
5. At 45+, none of this matters.
