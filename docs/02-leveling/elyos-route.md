# Elyos Route (Poeta → Verteron)

| Levels | Zones | Key points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–9 | Poeta: Daminu Forest, Clione Lake, Phoenix Sky Island, Migzatu Brigade Base | Wings at 5 |
| 10–13 | Western and Eastern Cantas Valley | Dawn Legion at 10, Nezekan board at 12, first gathering |
| 14–18 | Ellun River Marsh, Contaminated Marsh, Spirit's Rest, Ellun River Midstream, Anguish Hill | Sealed dungeon tier 15 |
| 19–22 | Verteron Fortress Ruins, Destroyed Abyss Gate | Fortress Ruins Infiltration (20), Kaisinel's Seal (21). **Stigma and Ascension at 22** |
| 23–31 | Southern Tolbas Forest, Pilgrim's Ridge, Forest of Exiles, Aullaeu Village | Krao Cave (22), stigma slot 2 at 27 |
| 32–38 | Altamia Canyon, Altamia Highlands, Rock Tomb Hill, Destroyed Temple Site, Contaminated Sanctum | Fire Temple expedition (37), third Ascension at 32 |
| 39–42 | Kraka Footpath, Drana areas, Scarlet Forest | Where the Drana Flows (40), Triniel board |
| 43–45 | Nahid Brigade Fortress, Garden of the Illusion God | Draupnir (45), story ends |

## Gathering and leather while following the route
See [Level Finder](./level-finder.mdx) for each band. The best loops:
- **Levels 10–13:** Orichalcum in Western and Eastern Cantas Valley, leather from Stonebeak Crestlich and Gentle Popoku.
- **Levels 18–30:** Ellun River Midstream (84 Orichalcum, 66 Sapphire) is your longest-lasting gathering zone.
- **Levels 19–21:** Verteron Fortress Ruins has Ruby (70) and Orichalcum (48), and the sealed dungeons you need for Ascension.
- **Levels 41–45:** Scarlet Forest Sapphire is a very short walk from its teleport statue.

## Side quests worth taking
- The Future of Odyle Collection (about level 16) and Payback Time (about level 30): accessory quests.
- Poacher's Base (Tolbas Forest): stronghold belt scroll stop.
- Level 45 bracelet quest: Souls Bound by Duty (bracelet, 100,000 bound Kina).

:::note[Conflict]
The leveling guide puts Kraka Footpath at 39–42, while Raid Relay's zone table lists it at 45.
:::
