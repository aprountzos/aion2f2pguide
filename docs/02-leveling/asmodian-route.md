# Asmodian Route (Ishalgen → Altgard)

| Levels | Zones | Key points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–9 | Ishalgen: Munihelle Forest, Tunapre Lake, Anturn Cliff, Azrakar Brigade Guard Camp | Wings at 6 |
| 10–14 | Dredgion Crash Site, Calderon Canyon, Nameless Cemetery | Finding Nemon (10), move to Altgard, Nezekan board at 12 |
| 15–21 | Silent Hill, Sanctum Outpost, Tranein Highland, Moslan Forest | Imminent Threat (20), Zikel board 20, **Stigma and Ascension at 22** |
| 22–28 | Uruthumheim, Purifying Forest | Krao Cave (22) |
| 29–32 | Dranactus | Land of Rifts (29), Vaizel board at 30, third Ascension at 32 |
| 32–35 | Battlescar Mound, Basfelt Ruins | |
| 35–40 | Fafnite Deposit, Western and Eastern Gribade Highland, Idun's Lake | Stigma slot 4 at 37, Triniel at 40 |
| 41–45 | Black Claw Village, Lagta Fortress, Impetusium Square | The High Priest Awakens (44), Draupnir (45) |

## Gathering and leather while following the route
See [Level Finder](./level-finder.mdx). The best loops:
- **Levels 10–20:** Dredgion Crash Site, 180 Orichalcum nodes plus Swarm and Lupyllini leather mobs. Asvata at Nameless Cemetery (64).
- **Levels 21–22:** Moslan Forest, Orichalcum (170), Asvata (52), Withered Armadon leather.
- **Levels 33–37:** Basfelt Ruins has the biggest single Ruby cluster (120). Fafnite Deposit has 180 Orichalcum and Giant Ore Insectoid leather.

## Side quests worth taking
- Blades Temporary Outpost (Moslan Forest): stronghold belt scroll stop.
- Level 45 bracelet quest: A Comrade's Fate (bracelet, 100,000 bound Kina).

:::note[Conflict]
One route guide puts Calderon Canyon at levels 10–14, Raid Relay lists it at 22. Low-level Contaminated Swarm (lvl 11) spawn there, which supports the early range. The Ruby promotion trip needs Calderon Canyon at Novice 50, see [Gathering Overview](../03-gathering/gathering-overview.md).
:::
