# Free-to-Play Notes

## The model
- Global Aion 2 is **free-to-play** (PC via Steam and NC PURPLE). Money comes from a shop (cosmetics and consumables), battle passes (**Daeva Pass**, free and paid track) and an optional **$15/month subscription**.
- Free players get the campaign, dungeons, crafting, leveling, PvP, the Abyss and the free pass track.

## What the subscription gates
| Feature | Free | Subscriber |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Buy from the Market | **No** (all sources agree) | Yes |
| List items on the Market | **Disputed** — see below | Yes |
| Odyle Energy cap | 560 | 840 |
| Abyss time (Lower, Middle Reshanta) | 7 h per week | 14 h per week |
| Shugo Festival keys cap | 12 | 21 |
| Quna to Kina exchange | No | Yes |

:::warning[Conflicting reports on listing]
One review of the Global version says free players **can list** items for Kina but cannot buy. Other reports (pre-launch, and one Kina guide) say free players can **neither list nor buy**. Test it in game before you plan around selling surplus.
:::

## What this means for you
1. **Do not plan on buying materials.** Guides that say "buy Raw Leather from the Market" assume a subscription. Farm it ([Raw Leather](../04-leather/raw-leather.md)).
2. Most of your Kina will be **bound Kina** (quests, Duty Missions, battle pass). Unbound Kina comes mainly from Market and exchange sales.
3. Gather and craft **for yourself**: your own armor and weapons, see [Crafting Professions](../05-crafting/crafting-professions.md).
4. Do not over-invest in gear you will replace, see [Enhancement and Upgrades](../06-gear/enhancement-and-upgrades.md).

## F2P habits by level
- **10–45:** gather nodes while questing, see [Gathering by Level](../03-gathering/gathering-by-level.mdx).
- **22+:** Odyle Energy starts to build. Spend it on cubes, do not let it sit at the cap.
- **45:** the cap is where the real game starts, see [After Level 45](../08-level-45/after-level-45.md).
