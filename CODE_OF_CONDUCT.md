# Code of Conduct

This project is for players helping players. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, comments and discussions, agrees to keep it a friendly and useful place. The approach is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).

## Our standard

Good behavior:
- Being kind and patient, especially with beginners and first-time contributors.
- Giving and accepting feedback on the work, not on the person.
- Assuming good faith, and asking before assuming a mistake was careless.
- Citing sources and admitting when something is unverified or wrong.
- Respecting that people play differently (free-to-play, subscribers, any faction or server).

Not acceptable:
- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Doxxing or sharing someone's private information, including other people's account or character details without consent.
- Spam, advertising, referral links, and links to gold-selling, boosting or account-selling services.
- Sexualized content, or content that is not appropriate for a general game guide.
- Trolling, or repeatedly arguing a point after a decision was made.
- Publishing others' copyrighted text or assets without permission.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and the published site) and to anyone representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently block anyone who does so. Typical steps: a friendly reminder, then a warning, then removal from the project.

**To report a problem,** use GitHub's private reporting (Security tab → *Report a vulnerability*, and title it "Conduct report") or contact the maintainer through the options on their GitHub profile: https://github.com/aprountzos. Reports are handled privately and in good faith, and the person reporting will not be named to the person reported without consent.

Maintainers who do not follow this code may be removed by the project owner.
